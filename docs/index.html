Screen an X-ray for fractures, and see why.

Upload an X-ray of a hand, leg, hip or shoulder. A ResNet-18 estimates how likely a fracture is, shows where it looked, and writes a short report for a radiologist to review. It does not diagnose. No image leaves your device.
